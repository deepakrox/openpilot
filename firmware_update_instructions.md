# Firmware Update Instructions

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for updating the openpilot firmware, including how to check for updates and troubleshoot common update issues.

## Checking for Updates

1. Ensure your device is connected to a stable internet connection.
2. From the home screen, tap the "Settings" icon.
3. Navigate to "Software Updates" in the settings menu.
4. Tap "Check for Updates" to see if a new firmware version is available.

## Updating the Firmware

If an update is available, follow these steps:

1. Tap "Download Update" to begin the download process.
2. Once the download is complete, tap "Install Update".
3. Your device will reboot and begin the installation process.
4. Do not disconnect power or turn off the device during the update.
5. After the update is complete, your device will restart automatically.

## Verifying the Update

To confirm that the update was successful:

1. Go to "Settings" > "About Device".
2. Check the "Firmware Version" to ensure it matches the latest version number.

## Troubleshooting Common Update Issues

### Update Fails to Download

If the update fails to download:

1. Check your internet connection and ensure it's stable.
2. Restart your device and try the download again.
3. If the problem persists, try connecting to a different Wi-Fi network.

### Update Fails to Install

If the update fails to install:

1. Ensure your device has sufficient battery charge (at least 50% recommended).
2. Restart your device and attempt the installation again.
3. If the issue continues, try performing a factory reset:
   - Go to "Settings" > "System" > "Reset Options" > "Factory Reset"
   - Note: This will erase all data on your device.

### Device Unresponsive After Update

If your device becomes unresponsive after an update:

1. Force restart the device by holding the power button for 10 seconds.
2. If the device still doesn't respond, connect it to a computer and use the recovery mode to reinstall the firmware.

## Reverting to a Previous Firmware Version

In some cases, you may need to revert to a previous firmware version:

1. Download the previous firmware version from the official openpilot website.
2. Follow the manual installation instructions provided with the firmware package.

Note: Reverting to a previous version should only be done as a last resort and may affect system stability.

## Getting Help

If you encounter any issues not covered in this guide, please contact our support team or visit the community forums for assistance.

Remember to always keep your firmware up to date to ensure the best performance and security for your openpilot system.
