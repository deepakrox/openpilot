# Supported Vehicles

This page provides information about the vehicles supported by our system, including specific requirements or limitations for each model, and how to check if your vehicle is compatible.

## Checking Vehicle Compatibility

To determine if your vehicle is compatible with our system, follow these steps:

1. Identify your vehicle's make, model, and year.
2. Check the list of supported vehicles below.
3. If your vehicle is not listed, or you're unsure about compatibility, please contact our support team for further assistance.

## Vehicle-Specific Requirements and Limitations

Some vehicles may have specific requirements or limitations when used with our system. Here are some important notes:

1. **Toyota Prius (2016-2018)**: Requires additional hardware installation for full functionality.
2. **Honda Civic (2016-2017)**: Limited to adaptive cruise control features only.
3. **Ford F-150 (2021)**: Compatibility limited to specific trim levels. Please check with support for details.
4. **Hyundai Kona Electric**: Requires latest software update from Hyundai to ensure compatibility.
